## Scope and interpretation

All eleven class source organizations were inspected: Censor, Conduit, Elementalist, Fury, Null, Shadow, Tactician, Talent, Troubadour, Beastheart, Summoner. The nine Heroes-book classes were spatial-keyword screened through levels 1–10, with candidate clauses read. Actual checked-in compendium ingestion is mostly levels 1–3, Shadow through 6; later named abilities also occur in the supporting-complication ability choices. Beastheart and Summoner hero/companion imports through level 3 are included, plus the level-4 resource upgrades directly referenced by runtime. Do not call those two classes non-V1. Their levels 4–10 were also screened, but unimported later abilities are excluded from the active list. The complete minion/fixture/companion statblock roster is not certified here; the monster auditor owns statblocks. Imported companion feature/ability files ARE included below.

Also screened: all 12 ancestry trait folders, 100 complications, kit signatures, common actions, perks, career/culture source organization and bounded starting-item actions. Titles and the wider treasure corpus were screened for candidates but are separated below because general inventory is explicitly unimplemented and no title catalog was found. A content reference proves exposure, not executable behavior. The appendix identifies checked-in catalog versus direct source references; neither is called automatic without specific code evidence.

Exclude ordinary chosen-target/range confirmation; generic movement allowance; ordinary area/aura membership, entry, exit and turn riders that need only existing selected-target/effect.members state. Parent verified `convex/lib/areas.ts`, `shared/resolve/areas.ts`, and `web/effect-instances.tsx` Add/Remove. The retained area candidates have EXTRA square counts, pairwise relations, physical terrain/overlap, or path history. A simple recipient list is not evidence of a new questionnaire. Some borderline rows explicitly explain that boundary.

## Priority findings and current handling

1. **Existing deterministic-after-claim mechanics:** Beastheart adjacency Ferocity, Summoner in-range death Essence, Elementalist typed-damage Essence, Tactician allied heroic Focus, Troubadour LoE natural-roll Drama and Conduit domain Piety already use `resource.claim` (`convex/lib/resourceOperations.ts:77`; `shared/resolve/heroicResourceGeneration.ts`). Amounts and limits are automated after player claim. Retain these as low-burden context, not missing mechanics.
2. **Existing modifier/response controls:** Mark's two LoE predicates use `exclude` (`shared/resolve/modifiers.ts:356–363`). Turn-trigger offers explicitly accept/pass with range confirmed by acceptance (`convex/lib/triggeredActions.ts:423`). No separate geometry modal is established. Other specific reaction rows remain source candidates unless named runtime proof is provided.
3. **Recurring fact needs beyond membership:** Our Hearts Your Strength counts nearby allies each turn; Polder Geist needs enemy-relative LoE/hidden/concealment; Shadow-Born needs concealment; Gnoll-Mauled needs turn-start adjacency; Lycanthropy needs moonlight and nearest-target/tie resolution; Edict of Disruptive Isolation adds adjacency only when judgment does not already satisfy its OR clause. Source exposure is not proof these passives currently auto-fire.
4. **Path/count followups:** Wall of Fire counts squares, explicitly manual in `shared/resolve/areas.ts:418`; Every Step…Death!, Debilitating Strike, Mind Snare, Impart Force, Phase Hurl and Stasis Field also depend on actual distance. No Dying on My Watch counts enemies approached along a path and is explicitly trigger-manual in V173. V176's actual-movement outcome limit applies; a push allowance is not actual displacement.
5. **Independent nonspatial blockers:** Beastheart companion and Summoner minion/squad effects are explicitly manual in their `shared/content/classes/*/abilities.ts`; Summoner cost payment can still be automatic. This is not solely a geometry issue and is not grounds for removing the hero's other clauses.

Historical slice notes cited below are evidence of intended/manual handling at that slice, not a substitute for current-code or live behavior proof. No whole-ability removal or new blanket deferral is recommended. The deterministic column describes separability in the rules; it does NOT assert all those portions currently execute.


## Strict core: spatial facts that change a mechanical result

Core means that a missing spatial fact changes a number, grants or removes a condition/modifier, determines a compulsory target, enables a triggered response, or ends an ongoing effect. It does **not** mean currently automated. Core status remains separately labelled catalog/referenced/source-only and runtime evidence is explicit.

Ordinary return placement, legal teleport/passenger/summon spots, direction-only movement permissions, and environmental instructions are in the boundary appendix. They are not counted as new automatic questionnaires. Mixed effects remain in core only for the material gating clause: for example Parry gates half damage, Bodyswap gates an edge, Magma Titan gates restrained, and Cult Victim gates 5 damage. Their ordinary placement clauses stay table instructions.

Status labels: **catalog** = exact source in checked-in compendium JSON; **referenced** = exact source path in shared/convex TypeScript; **source-only** = no such exposure evidence found. None alone proves executable behavior.
