# Natural 19/20 and double bane

Research date: 2026-09-14. Question: Q-R-1. A dedicated research subagent checked the pinned local
Steel Compendium at `fb83a789da8f0327a389c277a0c790b1648d5810`, including the Heroes source-order

## Finding and evidence

Natural 19/20 remaining tier 3 under ordinary edges and banes, including double bane, has strong
contextual source support:

- [Natural Roll](../../vendor/steel-compendium/en/unified/md/rule/dice/natural-roll.md) defines the
  natural result as the dice sum before characteristics and modifiers, and says natural 19/20 is
  “always a tier 3 result regardless of any modifiers.”
- [Power Rolls — When to Use Edges and Banes](../../vendor/steel-compendium/en/unified/md/rule/dice/power-roll.md)
  describes edges and banes as ways to “modify a roll.”
- [Bane](../../vendor/steel-compendium/en/unified/md/rule/dice/bane.md) changes a double-bane outcome
  down one tier rather than subtracting from the numerical total. Read with the unconditional
  natural-roll rule, natural 19/20 is the supported exception.
- [Natural 19 or 20: Success With a Reward](../../vendor/steel-compendium/en/unified/md/rule/dice/natural-19-20.md)
  expressly grants success with a reward on tests, including medium and hard tests. The
  [difficulty table](../../vendor/steel-compendium/en/unified/md/rule/test/test-difficulty.md) agrees.
  The original question incorrectly implied that the reward necessarily depends on resolving the
  tier-precedence question; tests have this separate natural-success rule.

No sentence explicitly names “natural 19/20 versus double bane.” The conclusion combines the
unconditional natural-roll wording with the description of edges/banes as modifications. The user
confirmed that interpretation. It is not a blanket conclusion about unrelated forced automatic-tier
rules or voluntarily choosing a lower outcome.

## Distinctions to preserve

Natural means the two d10s themselves sum to 19 or 20; a modified total of 19/20 does not qualify.
Ordinary edges and banes cap at two each before cancellation, so additional sources do not create
unlimited tier shifts. Their final tier cannot reduce a natural 19/20 below tier 3.

A [critical hit](../../vendor/steel-compendium/en/unified/md/rule/combat/critical-hit.md) has its own
qualifying ability-roll and main-action requirements. Tier 3 alone grants no extra action. The
voluntary downgrade rule expressly preserves a qualifying critical hit's extra action even when a
lower outcome is chosen. The present ruling settles ordinary edge/bane precedence, not those other
workflows.
